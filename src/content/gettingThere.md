## ✈️ Flying In

### Detroit Metropolitan Airport (DTW)

Most guests will fly into Detroit. It's about **45–60 minutes by car** from downtown Toledo.

- [Detroit Metro Airport ➡ Marriot Toledo](https://maps.app.goo.gl/2oHfiSNdoY9E7tXW6)
- [Airlines and destinations served from DTW](https://www.metroairport.com/flights/airlines)

> 💡 **Book early.** Our wedding falls on **Memorial Day weekend**, one of the busiest travel weekends of the year.

## 🚐 Detroit ➡ Toledo

We're working hard to make sure **nobody needs to drive to or from the wedding itself**, so you may not need a car at all. Here are some other options for getting from the airport to Toledo:

#### Limo Services:

Several private companies will ferry you from Detroit Airport to Toledo

- [Limo Toledo](https://www.limotoledo.com/pricing/airport-service-rates-one-way)
- [Motor City Limousine](https://motorcitylimousine.com/)

#### Airport shuttle (DTW ↔ Toledo)

[Froggy Transportation](https://www.froggytransportation.com) runs a scheduled shuttle between DTW and Toledo several times a day. Note that the Toledo stop is **Franklin Park Mall**, not downtown, so plan on a short Uber or Lyft to the hotel. Check their site for the current schedule and fares, and book ahead.

## 🎶 Staying for Detroit? (Sunday & Monday)

The celebration continues in **Detroit on Sunday and Monday**! Downtown Detroit is about an hour's drive north of Toledo. If you're joining us, we suggest staying in one of these neighborhoods:

- **New Center**
- **Milwaukee Junction**
- **Downtown Detroit**
- **Boston Edison**

> ⚠️ **Book Detroit lodging as early as you can.** That same weekend (May 29–31) Detroit is hosting the [Movement Music Festival](https://movementfestival.com), which draws huge crowds to downtown. Hotels and short-term rentals fill up fast and prices climb.
